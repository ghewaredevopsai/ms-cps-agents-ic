Portfolio positioning
We reduced equity modestly during June as large-cap valuations extended,
ending the quarter at 68% against a neutral 65%. The debt sleeve was
lengthened in duration following the softer inflation print.

Gold
The gold sleeve remains at approximately 5% of the portfolio. We continue to
hold it as a drawdown hedge rather than a return driver, and we have no
current intention of increasing it beyond 7% under any scenario.

Outlook
We expect range-bound equity markets through the second half, with the
fixed-income sleeve carrying more of the return burden than in 2025. The
fund remains positioned for a 5+ year horizon.

Ananya Krishnan, CFA - Fund Manager
