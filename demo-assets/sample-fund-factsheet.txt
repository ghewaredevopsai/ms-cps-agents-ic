1. FUND OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
The Northwind Multi-Asset Growth Fund is an open-ended multi-asset
allocation fund that invests across equity, fixed income, gold and
cash. Its objective is long-term capital growth with lower volatility
than a pure equity fund, by dynamically allocating across asset classes.
The fund is designed for long-horizon investors and institutional
allocators (including pension and retirement portfolios) seeking a
single diversified, professionally managed growth vehicle.

3. INVESTMENT STRATEGY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Core allocation to Indian large- and mid-cap equities for growth.
- Fixed income (government securities and high-grade corporate bonds)
  for stability and income.
- A gold sleeve (via sovereign gold bonds / gold ETFs) as an inflation
  and equity-drawdown hedge.
- Allocation is reviewed monthly and tilted based on valuation,
  interest-rate outlook and macro conditions.
- The fund does not use leverage and avoids unrated credit.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
================================================================

Q1. What is the fund's objective?
Long-term capital growth with lower volatility than a pure equity
fund, achieved by allocating dynamically across equity, debt, gold
and cash.

Q2. Is this fund suitable for a pension or retirement allocation?
It is designed for long-horizon investors, including institutional
and retirement portfolios, via the Institutional share class. As
with any equity-oriented fund, suitability depends on the investor's
risk tolerance and time horizon; a consultant should confirm fit
against the client's investment policy.

Q3. What is the benchmark and why?
The CRISIL Hybrid 35+65 Aggressive Index, which reflects a roughly
65% equity / 35% debt blend — a close match to the fund's target
asset mix, making it a fair yardstick for performance.

Q4. What returns has the fund delivered?
As of 30 June 2026 (annualised): 14.2% over 1 year, 15.6% over
3 years, 13.9% over 5 years, and 12.4% since inception — ahead of
the benchmark over each period. (Illustrative figures.)

Q5. What are the fees?
The total expense ratio is 1.18% per year for the Regular plan and
0.62% for the Direct plan. An exit load of 1% applies if you redeem
within 12 months.

Q6. How liquid is the fund? How do redemptions work?
It is open-ended, so units can be redeemed on any business day at the
applicable NAV. Redemptions within 12 months of purchase incur a 1%
exit load; after 12 months there is no exit load.

Q7. How much of the fund is in equities versus safer assets?
Currently 68% equity, 24% fixed income, 5% gold, and 3% cash. The
mix is reviewed monthly and can shift with market conditions.

Q8. What are the largest holdings?
The top holdings include HDFC Bank (6.2%), Reliance Industries
(5.1%), ICICI Bank (4.3%), Infosys (3.8%) and a government security,
7.18% GOI 2033 (3.5%). Financials and IT are the largest equity
sectors.

Q9. Does the fund use leverage or invest in risky credit?
No. The fund does not use leverage and avoids unrated credit,
holding only government securities and high-grade corporate bonds
in its fixed-income sleeve.

Q10. Why does the fund hold gold?
The ~5% gold sleeve (via sovereign gold bonds and gold ETFs) acts as
an inflation hedge and tends to diversify equity risk during
market drawdowns.

Q11. Who manages the fund?
Ananya Krishnan, CFA, who has run the fund since its 2016 inception,
supported by a team of sector analysts and a fixed-income strategist.

Q12. What is the minimum investment?
Rs 5,000 for the retail plan and Rs 1 crore for the Institutional
