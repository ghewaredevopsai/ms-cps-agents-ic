What this is, and what it is not. A watchlist. Every demonstration on the day is live, on screen, and explained as it happens — so nothing here is required preparation and there is nothing to install, build or sign up for. The point of watching first is narrower than that: the product has a lot of surface, and it is much easier to follow a live build if you have already seen somebody do it once. Watch the first four and stop. The rest are there for afterwards.
One caution that applies to every link below. Copilot Studio's interface has changed repeatedly, and it is still changing. Menus move, features get renamed, and a video recorded eight months ago will not match the product exactly. That is not a reason to skip them — watch for what is being built and why, not for where the buttons are. On the day you will see the current product.

What to watch for

One thing, in all of them. There is a moment in every video where the agent stops answering out of what it was given and reaches outside the conversation — opens a document, calls a flow, writes a file, sends something to a person. Everything easy is on one side of that line and everything valuable is on the other. The day is largely about how to cross it safely, and what has to be true before you do.

You will not be asked to build anything. There is no lab, no environment to prepare and no software to install. Every demonstration runs on screen, and you keep the full write-up of each one afterwards.
